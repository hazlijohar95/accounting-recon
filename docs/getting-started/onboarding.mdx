# Onboarding Your Company

After signing up, you'll complete an onboarding flow to configure Reconciled for your business. Choose between a traditional form wizard or an AI-powered conversational setup.

## Onboarding Methods

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Form Wizard">
    The classic 8-step form wizard walks you through each setting:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Company Name">
        Enter your registered business name. This appears on reports and exports.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Company Code">
        A unique identifier for your company (auto-generated from your name).

        Example: `ABC SDN BHD` → `ABC-001`
      </Step>

      <Step title="Industry">
        Select your business industry. This helps Reconciled suggest relevant categories and matching rules.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Currency">
        Choose your primary operating currency:
        - MYR (Malaysian Ringgit)
        - USD (US Dollar)
        - SGD (Singapore Dollar)
        - And 50+ other currencies
      </Step>

      <Step title="Fiscal Year">
        Set your financial year end date. Common options:
        - December 31 (calendar year)
        - March 31 (UK/Commonwealth)
        - June 30 (Australia)
        - Custom date
      </Step>

      <Step title="Tax Information">
        Enter your tax registration details:
        - Tax/GST registration number
        - Tax rate for your jurisdiction
      </Step>

      <Step title="Preferences">
        Configure display preferences:
        - Date format (DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD)
        - Number format (1,000.00 vs 1.000,00)
        - Theme (Light/Dark/System)
      </Step>

      <Step title="Review & Complete">
        Review all settings and click **Complete Setup** to enter the dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="AI Conversational">
    Let AI guide you through setup with natural conversation:

    1. Select **Set up with AI** on the onboarding screen
    2. The AI assistant asks questions one at a time
    3. Respond naturally—no forms to fill

    **Example conversation:**

    > **AI:** What's your company name?
    >
    > **You:** ABC Trading Sdn Bhd
    >
    > **AI:** Great! What currency do you primarily operate in?
    >
    > **You:** Malaysian Ringgit
    >
    > **AI:** And when does your fiscal year end?
    >
    > **You:** December
    >
    > **AI:** Perfect! I've set up ABC Trading with MYR currency and a December 31 fiscal year end. Ready to start?

    <Tip>
      AI onboarding is faster if you're comfortable with conversational interfaces. It extracts the same information as the form wizard.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Settings Reference

| Setting | Description | Can Change Later? |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Company Name | Your business name for reports | ✅ Yes |
| Company Code | Unique identifier | ❌ No |
| Industry | Business category | ✅ Yes |
| Currency | Default transaction currency | ✅ Yes |
| Fiscal Year | Financial year end | ✅ Yes |
| Tax Number | Registration number | ✅ Yes |
| Date Format | Display preference | ✅ Yes |
| Number Format | Display preference | ✅ Yes |

## Multi-Company Support

<Note>
  Multi-company support is coming soon. Currently, each account supports one company. To manage multiple companies, create separate accounts.
</Note>

## Changing Settings Later

All settings except Company Code can be modified after onboarding:

1. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Select the relevant settings page
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Save**

See [Company Settings](/settings/company) for detailed configuration options.
