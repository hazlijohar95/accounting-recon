# Quick Start Guide

This guide walks you through your first reconciliation—from signup to matched transactions.

## Prerequisites

- A bank statement (PDF, CSV, or image)
- Corresponding invoices or receipts
- An email address for signup

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Go to [reconciled.app](https://reconciled.app) and click **Get Started**.

    Sign up using:
    - Google or Microsoft account (recommended)
    - Email and password

    You'll be redirected to complete onboarding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Company">
    The onboarding wizard collects your company details:

    - **Company name** — Your business name
    - **Currency** — Default currency for transactions (MYR, USD, SGD, etc.)
    - **Fiscal year** — Your financial year end date

    <Tip>
      You can also use **AI onboarding** which asks questions conversationally instead of forms.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload Your First Files">
    From the dashboard, click **Upload** and drag your files:

    **Bank Statement**
    - PDF bank statement from your bank portal
    - CSV export from online banking
    - Screenshot or scanned image

    **Invoices/Receipts**
    - PDF invoices
    - Receipt images
    - Bulk CSV of invoice data

    Reconciled automatically categorizes files as cash (bank) or accrual (invoices).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Extracted Data">
    After upload, Reconciled extracts transaction data:

    - Transaction dates
    - Amounts and currencies
    - Descriptions and references
    - Vendor/customer names

    Verify the extraction is accurate. You can edit any field if needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run Reconciliation">
    Click **Reconcile** to start the 5-layer matching process:

    1. Exact matches appear first (same amount, same date)
    2. Window matches find transactions within date range
    3. Reference matching links invoice numbers
    4. Fuzzy matching catches partial description matches
    5. AI semantic matching understands context

    Each match shows a confidence percentage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Matches">
    The review screen shows all matches:

    | Status | Action Needed |
    |--------|---------------|
    | ✅ Auto-matched (90%+) | None, verified automatically |
    | 🔶 Suggested (70-89%) | Quick review, press A to approve |
    | ❓ Suspense (below 70%) | Manual matching required |

    **Keyboard shortcuts:**
    - `A` — Approve match
    - `R` — Reject match
    - `↑/↓` — Navigate between matches
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export Your Report">
    Once reconciliation is complete, export your results:

    - **Summary Report** — High-level reconciliation overview
    - **All Matches** — Complete match listing
    - **Differences** — Unmatched items only

    Export formats: CSV, Excel, or direct import to QuickBooks/Xero.
  </Step>
</Steps>
